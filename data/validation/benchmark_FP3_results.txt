FP-3 retrospective enrichment benchmark
---------------------------------------
Actives: 22 ChEMBL falcipain-3 inhibitors (IC50/Ki/Kd <= 10 uM; 3 covalent)
Decoys:  350 property-matched decoys
Docking: LigPrep -> Glide HTVS -> SP funnel into the falcipain-3 grid
Coverage: 16/22 actives and 115/350 decoys produced docked poses

Docked ligands (n=131): ROC-AUC 0.576 (95% CI 0.443-0.711); EF1% 0.00; EF5% 0.00
Full set, non-docking ligands assigned the worst score (n=372):
                        ROC-AUC 0.717 (95% CI 0.609-0.818); EF1% 16.91; EF5% 5.64
EF differences between the two treatments reflect that actives docked more often than decoys.
